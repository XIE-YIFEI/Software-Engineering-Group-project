How the check works

Consecutive sightings are compared: the distance between the two cameras divided by the time between the sightings gives the speed the vehicle would have needed. Faster than 250 km/h is impossible, so the same plate is probably on two vehicles (a cloned plate). Sightings at the same site (under 2 km apart) never count. The rule is alpr.registry.check_travel, the same one the pipeline uses.
